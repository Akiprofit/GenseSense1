Key Research Findings

Safety & Science

Approved GMO foods are as safe as conventional foods, according to major scientific organizations including the WHO, FDA, and National Academy of Sciences.

Documented Benefits

GMO crops have increased yields by an average of 22%, reduced pesticide use in many contexts, and improved farmer income in developing countries.

Legitimate Concerns

Valid questions exist about corporate consolidation, agricultural monoculture, equity in technology access, and long-term environmental impacts.

Values-Based Decision Making

Whether and how to use GMO technology depends on values about innovation, precaution, corporate power, and environmental stewardship—not science alone.

Our Research Approach

GeneSense takes a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary approach to GMO research:

  • ✓ Evidence-Based: We rely on peer-reviewed scientific research and data from major scientific organizations.
  • ✓ Balanced Perspective: We present multiple viewpoints and acknowledge legitimate concerns alongside documented benefits.
  • ✓ Nuanced Analysis: We distinguish between science (what research shows) and values (how to respond to that evidence).
  • ✓ Real-World Context: We examine how GMOs actually function in different regions and agricultural systems, not in theory alone.
  • ✓ Educational Focus: We aim to help students, educators, and the public understand this complex issue, not to persuade them toward a predetermined conclusion.
