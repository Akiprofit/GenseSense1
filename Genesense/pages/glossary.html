GMO Glossary

Ten key terms used in the research paper

Definitions below follow the key terms identified in the research paper and are intended as introductory explanations.

GMOs (Genetically Modified Organisms)

Organisms whose DNA has been deliberately altered using biotechnology.

Genetic Modification

The deliberate alteration of DNA using molecular tools.

Human Health

The physical and mental well-being of individuals and populations.

Society

Organized groups of people who share culture, values, and institutions.

Ethical Implications

Moral questions and value considerations that arise from the use of a technology or practice.

GMO Foods

Food or agricultural products derived from genetically modified organisms.

Food Safety

The condition of food being free from harmful contaminants, toxins, and allergens.

Biotechnology

The application of biological processes or organisms for purposes such as agriculture and medicine.

Biodiversity

The variety of living species and genetic variation within ecosystems.

Consumer Choice

The ability of consumers to select products in line with their needs, information, and values.

Source note: Glossary terms are based on the “Definition of Terms” section of the GeneSense research paper. They are simplified for website readers.
